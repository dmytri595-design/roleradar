# Buyer Outreach

**Subject:** CommitPulse — focused supplier-confirmation workflow SaaS

Hi,

I’m offering CommitPulse, a small B2B workflow product built around one procurement pain point: getting reliable delivery and quantity confirmations from suppliers without endless manual chasing.

The current MVP lets a buyer import open POs, group follow-ups by supplier, preview a no-login supplier request, capture confirmations or exceptions, keep an audit trail, and export the normalized result.

It is deliberately positioned as a thin layer beside an existing ERP rather than another procurement suite.

Current stage: working MVP, pre-launch, pre-revenue.
Asking price: $4,900 one-time.

Live demo: https://commitpulse-demo.vercel.app
Repository: https://github.com/dmytri595-design/roleradar/tree/commitpulse-sale-v1.0

The production roadmap is straightforward: signed supplier tokens, email reminders, shared persistence, and ERP connectors.
