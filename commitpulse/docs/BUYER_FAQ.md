# Buyer FAQ

### Does CommitPulse replace my ERP?
No. The product is designed to sit beside an ERP and consume an export first.

### Can suppliers use it without an account?
The demo supplier page is no-login. Production should issue a signed, expiring request token.

### Does the demo send real emails?
No. Delivery is simulated locally.

### What does the buyer get back?
A normalized CSV containing PO number, supplier, quantity, required date, confirmed date, status and notes.

### Is there customer data?
No. Demo data is synthetic.

### What is the current business stage?
Working MVP / pre-launch / pre-revenue.
