# Cutloom — valuation notes

## Suggested asking price

**$4,900 USD one-time**

This is positioned as an early-stage creator-tool acquisition asset rather than a revenue multiple. The price is based on the value of a polished niche workflow, working source code, a public demo and an extension-ready architecture, while explicitly discounting for zero revenue, no customer base and no production media integrations.

## Buyer fit

- indie hackers building creator SaaS;
- video agencies that want an internal planning layer;
- social-content studios;
- marketing teams building short-form content pipelines;
- founders adding collaboration, AI generation or cloud storage on top of a working UX.

## Value drivers

The differentiator is interaction design: Cutloom presents the product as a miniature editing environment rather than another CRUD dashboard. The MVP now also accepts real local image/video/audio assets, persists them in-browser, and can render media-aware WebM drafts without a backend.

## What is not claimed

No customers, MRR, ARR, licensing revenue, production media library, paid AI usage, or enterprise contracts are claimed.
