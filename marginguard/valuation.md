# MarginGuard - Pricing Rationale (2026)

## Recommended ask

**$4,900 USD - one-time acquisition**

## Why this product category

Current 2026 industry coverage continues to highlight project-margin erosion from scope creep and the importance of comparing planned hours with actual delivery cost while work is still in progress. This makes project profitability monitoring a concrete B2B operational problem rather than a generic productivity feature.

## Market evidence

Acquire.com says valuation for SaaS businesses is generally anchored to profit once a company has credible earnings, while guidance for many SaaS businesses sits around 3x-5x annual profit or 1x-3x annual revenue depending on the business. For a zero-revenue MVP, those formulas are not appropriate; an asset-style price is more honest.

Acquire.com's seller guidance also indicates that pre-revenue listings are more selective and need to be useful, niche and well presented. MarginGuard is designed around that requirement: it has a specific agency/consultancy workflow, a live demo and a clear buyer use case.

## Product-value argument

The asking price is supported by:

- a complete working MVP;
- a clear project-profitability workflow;
- deterministic calculations that a buyer can audit;
- configurable business thresholds;
- alert and review lifecycle;
- data import/export;
- portable reports;
- zero-backend demo architecture;
- a straightforward route to integrations and recurring revenue.

## Price constraints

The price is discounted because the MVP has:

- no customers;
- no MRR;
- no production authentication;
- no backend persistence;
- no native time-tracking / PM integrations;
- no billing system.
